# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Clinic and hospital receptionists, organization administrators, and operations staff working at a busy front desk. They need to configure clinical services, manage daily schedules and queues, and book or change appointments quickly without technical knowledge.

## Product Purpose

Provide one protected workspace for manual and AI-assisted reception. Success means staff can configure departments, doctors, weekly capacity, and exceptions; identify callers and patients; and safely book, find, reschedule, cancel, and complete appointments through the same operational system.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and trustworthy. The interface should feel premium through disciplined spacing, clear states, and reliable workflows rather than decoration.

## Anti-references

Avoid oversized black headings, decorative dashboard heroes, dense technical configuration, excessive cards, vague status labels, and interfaces that resemble generic AI-generated admin templates.

## Design Principles

1. Put the receptionist's next action close to the relevant patient or schedule.
2. Show committed database state clearly; never imply a booking succeeded before confirmation.
3. Keep high-frequency workflows compact and keyboard-friendly.
4. Reveal advanced configuration progressively while preserving safe defaults.
5. Use one consistent vocabulary for doctors, departments, sessions, slots, tokens, and visit states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA design practices, including readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, explicit labels, reduced-motion support, and usable desktop and tablet layouts. Do not claim certification without a formal audit.
